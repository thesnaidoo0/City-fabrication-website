Our services

Insulation, cladding and fabrication for industrial projects.

From thermal performance to final weatherproofing, we support the full scope.

01 — Thermal insulation

Hot and cold insulation systems

We install insulation systems to reduce heat loss or heat gain, protect personnel and support process temperature control.

  • Pipe insulation and lagging
  • Boilers and furnace insulation
  • Tanks, vessels and process equipment
  • Plant room insulation
  • HVAC duct insulation
  • Cold-room and refrigeration applications
Request pricing
02 — Industrial cladding

Protective metal finishes

We fabricate and install weatherproof and mechanical protection over insulation systems.

  • Aluminium cladding
  • Stainless steel cladding
  • Galvanised cladding
  • Pipe muffs and lobster bends
  • Flange and valve boxes
  • Tank, vessel and equipment cladding
03 — Sheet metal fabrication

Custom components made to fit

Our workshop produces sheet-metal items for insulation, HVAC, roofing and industrial applications.

  • Bends and elbows
  • Muffs and pipe cladding
  • Tees and reducers
  • Flange boxes, valve boxes and strainer boxes
  • Square-to-round transitions and ducting
  • Gutters, flashings and custom sheet-metal items
