About City Fabrication

Industrial workmanship backed by practical experience.

We combine material supply, fabrication and site installation to support industrial and commercial insulation projects.

Our company

Thermal insulation and sheet metal capability under one roof.

City Fabrication and Sheet Metal Works (Pty) Ltd is based in Lenasia, Gauteng and serves clients across South Africa.

Our work includes hot and cold insulation, industrial cladding, sheet metal fabrication and the supply of insulation materials, metal sheeting, fasteners and accessories.

We support projects ranging from day-to-day maintenance to shutdowns, plant upgrades and new installations.
